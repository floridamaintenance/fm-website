Power Scrubbing

Big Floors, Minimal Downtime

Power scrubbing is one of the least disruptive ways to clean concrete. Our ride-on scrubbers use rotating brushes on a 5-foot deck and biodegradable chemicals to lift dust, oil, stains and debris.

Because they scrub and vacuum in a single pass, your floors are dry and back in service almost immediately — with minimal interruption to your operation.

  • Cleans large areas fast
  • No pre-sweeping required
  • Drive-mounted, single-pass operation
  • Floors dry within minutes
