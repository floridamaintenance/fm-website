Highway Wall Cleaning

Cleaner Walls for Roads, Ramps and Transportation Corridors

Highway walls collect exhaust film, tire dust, mildew, road grime, algae and construction residue. We clean concrete barrier walls, sound walls, retaining walls, bridge approaches, ramp walls and tunnel walls with specialty-built equipment we fabricate and adapt in-house for heavy exterior work.

Our Miami-based crew understands transportation sites: lane access, traffic timing, night work, staged cleanups and the coordination needed to keep roadway properties moving.

  • Barrier walls, sound walls and retaining walls
  • Tunnel walls, ramp walls, bridge approaches and medians
  • In-house specialty equipment for wall washing and pressure cleaning
  • One-time cleanup or recurring corridor maintenance
