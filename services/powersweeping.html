Power Sweeping

The Fastest Way to a Cleaner Property

Sweeping is the single most effective way to keep a property looking cared-for. Our truck-mounted sweepers pull up debris and surface dirt from lots, garages, roadways, bridges and tunnels — leaving your site spotless without leaving a mark.

If it's vehicle-accessible, there's a good chance our equipment can handle it. For tighter, more restricted areas, we'll pair sweeping with one of our pressure-cleaning options.

  • Low-clearance machines (6'4") for garages
  • Active dust control on every machine
  • Sharpco shoes — no road marking
  • Local scheduling for active South Florida properties
