Parking Lot Cleaning

A Cleaner Lot, Top to Bottom

Parking lots are the first thing tenants, customers and inspectors see. Ours is a combined approach: power sweeping to clear debris and dirt, pressure cleaning for gum, grime and stains, and hot-water treatment for oil spots — so the whole lot reads clean, not just swept.

We service lots across Miami-Dade and Broward on recurring monthly, biweekly or weekly programs, scheduled at night or off-hours around your traffic. One-time deep cleans and post-construction cleanups available.

  • Retail, office, industrial and HOA lots
  • Sweeping + pressure washing combined
  • Oil stain and gum removal
  • Night and off-hours scheduling

Our Approach

Sweeping and Pressure Washing, Combined

  • Power sweeping to clear loose debris before pressure washing
  • Hot-water treatment for oil and grease stains
  • Self-contained water tanks where hookups are limited
  • Backpack blowers for curbs, corners and under parked cars
