Pressure Cleaning

Bring Back That Like-New Look

Dirt, mildew, oil and tire marks build up fast on high-traffic concrete — and they drag down how your whole property looks. Our commercial-grade pressure cleaning strips it all away, restoring your surfaces and protecting them from long-term wear.

Whether the job is a parking garage, storefront, sidewalk or busy South Florida shopping center, you get quick response, professional crews and a clean finish.

  • Hot- & cold-water washing
  • Self-contained water tanks — no hookup needed
  • Surface cleaners for even, streak-free results
  • Non-marking, no-damage equipment
