Parking Garage Cleaning

Clean, Safer Garages Without Disrupting Traffic

Parking garages collect oil, tire marks, dust, trash and standing grime fast. We combine pressure cleaning, power scrubbing and sweeping to restore concrete decks, ramps, curbs, columns and pedestrian areas.

Our crews work around active South Florida properties with low-clearance equipment, self-contained water capacity and scheduling options for nights, weekends or recurring maintenance.

  • Oil stain and tire-mark treatment
  • Low-clearance garage equipment
  • One-time or recurring service plans
