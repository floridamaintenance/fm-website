About Florida Maintenance

Over 15 years helping South Florida properties stay clean, safe and presentable.

Florida Maintenance is a Miami-based property maintenance company with over 15 years of experience serving South Florida. Our goal is simple: be the local crew property owners and managers can call when the job needs real equipment, reliable scheduling and direct accountability.

We handle the heavy-duty cleaning that South Florida properties demand — pressure washing and power sweeping for commercial and residential parking lots and garages, plus retail centers, warehouses, sidewalks, roadways and tunnels. With the right equipment, trained crews and flexible scheduling, our hassle-free service keeps your facility looking its best for the clients, customers, tenants, investors and inspectors who matter most.

From a single deep clean to a standing maintenance program, we treat every property like it's our own — showing up on time, working around your hours, and leaving the site cleaner than we found it.
